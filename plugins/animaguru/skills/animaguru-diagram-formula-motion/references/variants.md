# Timed concept and simulation diagrams

Choose one coherent treatment from the brief; these are visual/motion recipes, not mandatory stories. They apply to Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Semantic identity

Give objects, terms and relationships stable IDs. A term entering a formula matches the object or variable it denotes; labels and connector endpoints follow geometry. Reveal enough context before changing a parameter.

## Timed process simulation

Define state from absolute time or bake a checked event schedule. Token buckets, flows or measured recipe quantities can be shown with this approach. Topic-specific physical/numerical truth needs independent source/calculation checks.

## Reading and numerical checks

Pause moving geometry when required text needs attention. Check domains, units, graph discontinuities and term transformations. Do not imply equality or causal relationships just because shapes animate smoothly into one another.

Use the bundled absolute-time helpers in `src/studies/timing.ts` where appropriate. Keep actual resources and cues in the timeline, then inspect cold seeks, full-speed motion and exported frames. The shared video skill governs interviews, generation scope and delivery.
