# Typography variants

Choose one coherent treatment from the brief; these are visual/motion recipes, not mandatory stories. They apply to Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Swiss editorial

Build a modular grid and optical hierarchy with one disciplined type system and controlled accent. Snap arrivals to chosen cues using deliberate easing; lines may draw linearly. Grid modules should give words mass, not divide the frame into dozens of tiny labels.

## Structural letterforms

Use actual glyph paths for words that become supports, apertures or surfaces. Keep counters and recognizable forms intact. Unrelated paths need matched edges or occluded handoffs; do not interpolate arbitrary vertices and claim a clean word morph.

## Impact poster and lyric treatment

Use concise word groups, deliberate print offsets and matched masks. A poster impact is an option, not a fixed manifesto. Lyrics align to the actual vocal phrases; speech should remain readable before it becomes geometry. Distinguish phrase cues from musical downbeats.

Use the bundled absolute-time helpers in `src/studies/timing.ts` where appropriate. Keep actual resources and cues in the timeline, then inspect cold seeks, full-speed motion and exported frames. The shared video skill governs interviews, generation scope and delivery.
