# Ambient & Seamless Loop

Build a mood or display loop with continuous motion at its boundary.

Fold this missing information into the shared quick interview: **What mood, loop length, audio, motion tolerance and display context should the loop serve?** Reuse supplied answers. Save the chosen angle, constraints and evidence in `production/brief.md`; a workflow is not a fixed story template.

- Choose a periodic time model or a fully occluded handoff. Check position, opacity, light, particles, source-video time and movement velocity at the boundary.
- Audio loops need their own seam check; a silent visual loop does not establish soundtrack continuity. Derive procedural variation from periodic functions or a baked loop schedule.
- Keep display and embed behavior appropriate: autoplay muted where supported, preserve an accessible way to start/unmute, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
