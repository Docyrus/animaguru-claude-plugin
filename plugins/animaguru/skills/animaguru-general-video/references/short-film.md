# Original Short Film

Create a film from a subject, character or idea with an intentional ending.

Fold this missing information into the shared quick interview: **What subject, tone, characters, sound/dialogue and ending intent should guide the film?** Reuse supplied answers. Save the chosen angle, constraints and evidence in `production/brief.md`; a workflow is not a fixed story template.

- Write a treatment and shot choices appropriate to the subject. A hook, a turn, dialogue or a four-camera-move quota is not universally required.
- Give meaningful actions anticipation, action and follow-through. Maintain prop contact, eyelines and spatial relationships through camera moves.
- Use the selected style as material grammar. Sound may be narration, dialogue, ambience, foley, music or silence according to the brief.

Completion: Readable performance, intentional pacing, coherent space and an ending that serves the brief. Use `animaguru-video` for the common production, budget, review and MP4 delivery contract.
