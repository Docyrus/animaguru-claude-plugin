# Character finish and cadence

Choose one coherent treatment from the brief; these are visual/motion recipes, not mandatory stories. They apply to Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Rubber-hose movement

Continuous noodle-like limbs and elastic action can make objects expressive. Sample drawing poses deliberately while the camera remains smooth. Keep stroke weight stable in screen space and preserve a clear silhouette; no existing character shapes or mandatory jazz/comedy sequence.

## Mid-century limited animation

Use separate off-register fill/line plates and held bodies with active hands, eyes and mouth. Keep hand contact with props and build anticipation/action/settle from poses. Classroom narration and instructional scene order are choices from the brief.

## Cel and dialogue performance

Define pose, mouth and expression sets with explicit cadence, plus prepared painted backgrounds. A held cel can still act through eyes or a carefully timed gesture. Lip synchronization requires recorded speech timing or verified mouth cues; a generic talk loop is not lip sync.

Use the bundled absolute-time helpers in `src/studies/timing.ts` where appropriate. Keep actual resources and cues in the timeline, then inspect cold seeks, full-speed motion and exported frames. The shared video skill governs interviews, generation scope and delivery.
