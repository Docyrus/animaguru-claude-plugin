# Cutaways and focus/context

Choose one coherent treatment from the brief; these are visual/motion recipes, not mandatory stories. They apply to Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Cut line before opening

Represent the removable skin separately from the interior. Draw the cut boundary, move the skin along its normal, then reveal a hatched rim. Parts, camera and callout anchors share a single projection.

## Tracked inset and emphasis

A leader grows from a known part anchor before an inset opens. Retain context with selective desaturation instead of blurring everything except the subject. Composite objects need ordered depth groups; overhangs may require explicit depth bias.

## Geometry review

Check outward face orientation, painter sorting and valid interior coverage. A supposedly solid moving object should not become transparent merely to avoid occlusion. Keep quantities and diagram conventions grounded in the source.

Use the bundled absolute-time helpers in `src/studies/timing.ts` where appropriate. Keep actual resources and cues in the timeline, then inspect cold seeks, full-speed motion and exported frames. The shared video skill governs interviews, generation scope and delivery.
