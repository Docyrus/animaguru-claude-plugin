# Portal Zoom

Nested compositions, matched apertures and continuous scale changes through layered scenes with planned parallax. Subject, audience, emotional tone and story come from the user's brief. This style supports Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Establish direction

Follow sibling `animaguru-video` for purpose → style → compact interview before new production. Reuse explicit choices and completed briefs; honor a requested interview skip. Share [the style gallery](https://animaguru.com/animation-styles#portal-zoom). Fold missing visual questions into the shared interview:

- Which material and aperture motifs fit the film?
- Should it loop, and how intense should the camera motion feel?

## Build the look and movement

Read `pixijs` and the relevant Graphics, Text, Container, Assets or Mesh skills. Read `animaguru` before generating assets; discover current models and estimates, respect the user's budget, and reuse completed media. Essential text and exact numerical geometry belong in authored canvas content.

- Prepare the next world inside the portal and match camera/framing when the aperture fills the frame. Backgrounds need overscan and filled hidden regions.
- Logarithmic scale progression can produce even perceived zoom speed. Segment timing comes from the film, not a fixed object list or song structure.
- Bound zoom by texture resolution and compute nested scenes from time. At a loop, match both composition and motion direction; a matching still alone does not prove a seamless loop.

Use the [production recipe](references/recipe.md) for the bundled original study and port-specific decisions. It is a technique example, not a prescribed film. Keep motions a function of absolute seconds; separate drawing cadence from camera/light cadence when the material calls for it. Prepare assets locally, preserve async seeking and register actual resource/cue times. Keep essential type editable and localized.

## Review and delivery

Inspect portal edges, maximum zoom resolution and disocclusion; scene handoff continuity and arbitrary seeks. Check representative full-resolution frames, moving playback, desktop/mobile fit and cold seeks.
