# Vector performance variants

Choose one coherent treatment from the brief; these are visual/motion recipes, not mandatory stories. They apply to Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Pictogram acting

Store joint poses and props in data; choose one clearly readable action per visual focus. Use a consistent figure scale, stroke family and geometry grid. A cue table can schedule contacts and releases without prescribing a numbered sports or instruction story.

## Hard-shadow panels

Use opaque flat shapes and a deliberate offset silhouette for depth. Keep shadow direction and width consistent. Layer transitions can use grid bands, matched shapes or occlusion rather than swapping whole unrelated slides.

## Rhythm and microgame panels

Call/response, round cards, timers and replay are optional workflow tools. If used, drive them from the same cue table and make each action/result readable. Do not force escalating difficulty, lives or a fixed frame-rate ladder on every vector film.

Use the bundled absolute-time helpers in `src/studies/timing.ts` where appropriate. Keep actual resources and cues in the timeline, then inspect cold seeks, full-speed motion and exported frames. The shared video skill governs interviews, generation scope and delivery.
