# Stroke deadlines and continuous line

Choose one coherent treatment from the brief; these are visual/motion recipes, not mandatory stories. They apply to Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Deadline-based writing

Measure authored centerline lengths and schedule strokes into explicit time windows. Give short dashes/dots suitable duration floors so a many-segment line does not consume the whole shot. Marker texture and width belong in board coordinates.

## Erase and rewrite

A deliberate erase path can reveal a revision; track which marks it should remove. Ghost traces and moving board props are optional material cues. Keep the camera and drawing connected through a shared world transform.

## Continuous-line variant

Plan path topology before adding a camera. Connect chapters along natural visible lines, with tangent-continuous joins and curvature-aware slowing. Inspect the complete final path early: stray connectors read as unintended features. The pen need not be a visible hand.

Use the bundled absolute-time helpers in `src/studies/timing.ts` where appropriate. Keep actual resources and cues in the timeline, then inspect cold seeks, full-speed motion and exported frames. The shared video skill governs interviews, generation scope and delivery.
