## Build the look and movement

Read `pixijs` and the relevant Graphics, Text, Container, Assets or Mesh skills. Read `animaguru` before generating assets; discover current models and estimates, respect the user's budget, and reuse completed media. Essential text and exact numerical geometry belong in authored canvas content.

- Choose a process before drawing. Copperplate uses swelling dark hatches; relief uses light gouges in dark masses; color woodblock uses separate keyed plates.
- Hatching follows form and remains local to moving pieces. Cache the geometry, schedule reveal by stable mark IDs, and test line density at delivery scale.
- Prepare underpainting/solid areas before fine marks so early frames read. Do not reduce all three processes to the same noise overlay.
