# Stop Motion Objects

Tactile tabletop objects and deliberate held poses assembled with attached contact shadows in a 2.5D treatment. Subject, audience, emotional tone and story come from the user's brief. This style supports Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Establish direction

Follow sibling `animaguru-video` for purpose → style → compact interview before new production. Reuse explicit choices and completed briefs; honor a requested interview skip. Share [the style gallery](https://animaguru.com/animation-styles#stop-motion-objects). Fold missing visual questions into the shared interview:

- What tactile material and tabletop scale fit?
- Do you have prepared views/poses or want generated assets?

## Build the look and movement

Read `pixijs` and the relevant Graphics, Text, Container, Assets or Mesh skills. Read `animaguru` before generating assets; discover current models and estimates, respect the user's budget, and reuse completed media. Essential text and exact numerical geometry belong in authored canvas content.

- Choose a material and pose cadence; preserve the same pieces and local texture across holds. Keep camera motion separately sampled.
- Use prepared views or projected flat forms for the current 2.5D tier; procedural physical bricks, arbitrary orbit and real rigid-body contact remain deferred.
- Base shadows and landing cues on contact points. A clay treatment is an original extension, not a copied upstream clay renderer.

Use the [production recipe](references/recipe.md) for the bundled original study and port-specific decisions. It is a technique example, not a prescribed film. Keep motions a function of absolute seconds; separate drawing cadence from camera/light cadence when the material calls for it. Prepare assets locally, preserve async seeking and register actual resource/cue times. Keep essential type editable and localized.

## Review and delivery

Inspect texture stability and consistent pose identity; contacts, piece clearance and material-appropriate cadence. Check representative full-resolution frames, moving playback, desktop/mobile fit and cold seeks. Follow `animaguru-video` for production records, audio mixing and HTML5 delivery; export MP4 when requested. Describe the actual 2D/2.5D rendering method accurately. Physically rendered 3D remains deferred.
