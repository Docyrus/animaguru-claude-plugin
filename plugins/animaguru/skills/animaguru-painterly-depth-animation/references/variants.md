# Painting material recipes

Choose one coherent treatment from the brief; these are visual/motion recipes, not mandatory stories. They apply to Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Watercolor and ink wash

Prepare stroke geometry or masks with pressure, wetness and dry edges. Coverage multiplies onto paper; reveal structure before detail. Cache completed objects and keep dry-brush gaps stable along normalized stroke length. A moving painting and a procedural stroke engine are different production choices.

## Crayon and wax resist

Anchor paper tooth to the page, with wax coverage or hatching in surface space. A white-wax reserve appears as a wash passes over it. Restrict line boil to intended drawn content and keep camera/light motion smooth.

## Impasto and pen/wash

For thick-paint effects, use value-planned shapes and strokes with consistent raking light; prepared texture relief is a 2D approximation. For pen/wash, ink arrives in stroke order and pigment blooms through a seeded arrival mask; wash edges follow actual silhouettes, not bounding rectangles.

Use the bundled absolute-time helpers in `src/studies/timing.ts` where appropriate. Keep actual resources and cues in the timeline, then inspect cold seeks, full-speed motion and exported frames. The shared video skill governs interviews, generation scope and delivery.
