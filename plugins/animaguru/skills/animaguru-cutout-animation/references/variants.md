# Paper mechanisms and graphic scenery

Choose one coherent treatment from the brief; these are visual/motion recipes, not mandatory stories. They apply to Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Fold, cut and unfold

Prepare the fold axis and back face. A mirrored pattern multiplies through a hinge/mesh transformation rather than merely sliding duplicate cards. Preserve holes, scissor edges and paper thickness during the transition.

## Illuminated paper depth

Give each layer a prepared alpha silhouette, depth and light response. Layered tint/transmission can suggest a lightbox in 2.5D. Full physical lamps, free camera orbit and real cast shadows remain deferred.

## Typographic scenery

Use letters or words as measured scene structures, with original glyph geometry and stable cut edges in local coordinates. Puppets may move in held drawings while the camera moves continuously. Spy chases, festivals, mandatory red and children's stories are optional subjects.

Use the bundled absolute-time helpers in `src/studies/timing.ts` where appropriate. Keep actual resources and cues in the timeline, then inspect cold seeks, full-speed motion and exported frames. The shared video skill governs interviews, generation scope and delivery.
