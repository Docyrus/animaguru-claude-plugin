# Cosmic four-color comic

Offset color plates, spotted blacks, feathered hatching, energy and exaggerated perspective.

## Material and construction

Draw color and ink separately. Halftone color plates can be offset about (4,3) px; composite a white-filled, black-ink pass with multiply. Make a dark crescent by subtracting a translated part from itself, then add a wider clipped hatching band. Source contours were about 6 px, hatching about 2.6 px at 9 px spacing.

## Palette

Cream paper #F4ECD6, CMYK-like flats, strong black and energetic yellow/orange.

## Motion

Animate clusters of black energy circles: denser and larger at the center, changing at about 12 fps. Let foreshortened hands or a prop lead speed lines, and reserve extruded onomatopoeia for an impact. A reaction inset can follow the main action.

## Transition

A comic panel shift, energy burst or angular impact wipe.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Letter bursts can cover faces. Split a planet ring into back and front arcs. The upstream sample remained closer to clean pop art: more forceful foreshortening, block-shaped fingers and muscular tension are needed for this specific reference language.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
