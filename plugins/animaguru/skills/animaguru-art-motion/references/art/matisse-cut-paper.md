# Gouache cut paper

Scissor edges, saturated sheets, pinned shadows and organic cut shapes.

## Material and construction

Sample contours every 14–24 px with about ±2–2.5 px seeded edge variation, then join with straight scissor segments. Build seaweed from a tapered stem and alternating round capsule lobes, unioned before fill and shadow. Add a short paper shadow around (5,6) px with blur 4 and .3 alpha. Keep gouache brush texture restrained.

## Palette

Cobalt #1D4FB8 / #4F95DC, green #1F9A5A, yellow, black, white and a small red accent.

## Motion

Pinned seaweed rotates around its base, paper edges curl, a bird wing flips and a loose scrap turns as it falls. ScaleX can approach zero during a flip, but preserve a coherent path and avoid the subject face.

## Transition

A colored sheet tiles or sweeps over the next composition.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Width-modulating a spine produced caterpillars rather than lobed seaweed in the source. Heavy long brush marks can overwhelm the clean cut-paper treatment. Place type on an actual contrasting paper piece when the background is saturated.
