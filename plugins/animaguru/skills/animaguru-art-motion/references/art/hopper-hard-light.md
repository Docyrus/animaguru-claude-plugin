# Quiet realism and hard sunlight

Architectural stillness, warm sunlight and sharply bounded cool shadow.

## Material and construction

Cache complete lit and shaded versions, then reveal the lit version with a moving hard-edged light polygon. Include the subject in both treatments so light really crosses the face and clothes. Shadows follow the current pose. Add low-contrast vertical oil texture rather than obvious marbling.

## Palette

Cool shade around #5D8679 and floor #5B3D2B; sunlight #EFCF8F and floor #C98B52.

## Motion

A sunlight patch crosses the room, a cord sways and a few dust particles appear only within the beam. Let an understated human action carry the scene. The source used about 95 px of light travel in 1.2 s as a starting point.

## Transition

A hard sunshine edge reveals the next space.

## Failure cases and adaptation

A fixed overlay on an unlit character looks pasted on. Strong horizontal texture reads as marbling; a quarter-resolution soft-light texture near .2 alpha was subtler than .35. Keep the subject readable in shadow; stillness is a valid part of this treatment.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
