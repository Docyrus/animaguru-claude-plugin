# Radiant line figures

Bold equal-weight contours, flat colors, dancing silhouettes and radiating action marks.

## Material and construction

Use roughly 13 px rounded black contours at 1080p, flat fills and a simple ground. Union capsule limbs before outlining to avoid holes at joints. Keep internal detail sparse and the contour weight consistent across figures, props and action marks.

## Palette

Yellow #FFD51C, green #16A54A, black and clear red/blue/pink accents.

## Motion

Hard-cut among four dance poses at about 8 fps rather than interpolating them smoothly. Add short radiating marks with stepped angles. Let objects squash around contact points on selected beats, with slight phase differences; a heart or small crawling figure can provide a secondary loop.

## Transition

A bold figure silhouette or radiating shape becomes a portal.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Fine hair lines contradict equal-weight drawing and may cross a moving face. Rounded limb endcaps must join without gaps. The source figure proportions were not fully characteristic, so test silhouette proportions before adding more activity.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
