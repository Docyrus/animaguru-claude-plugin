# Renaissance sfumato

Soft edges, modeled volume, aerial perspective and glazed oil texture.

## Material and construction

Paint perspective architecture and a cool receding landscape, then cache a lightly softened room. Render characters into a separate layer: a soft pass plus a clear pass preserves faces. The upstream revision reduced character blur from 2.6 to 1.6 px and raised the clear pass from .38 to .82 after the subjects became less legible than the distant view. Add restrained canvas, crackle, warm glaze and vignette.

## Palette

Umber and muted red indoors, blue-gray distant hills, a warm window-light pool; keep floor contrast subordinate to the subject.

## Motion

A ribbed ornithopter flaps outside, water catches light, birds cross and dust moves through a cached soft light shaft. Wing spars and radiating ribs communicate the mechanism better than a paper-plane silhouette.

## Transition

Travel through the arched window or use a soft light reveal.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Dense directional strokes drift toward post-impressionism. Over-dark inner contours make pale bodies look glassy. Cache blurred beams rather than painting hard light polygons each frame. Atmospheric softness should preserve the focal plane.
