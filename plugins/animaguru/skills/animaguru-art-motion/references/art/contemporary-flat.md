# Contemporary textured flat illustration

Clean silhouettes, regional stipple and expressive timing.

## Material and construction

Compose bold flat regions with little or no outline. Add seeded stipple whose density varies within a shape, then cache it. Build expressive pose targets appropriate to the subject; use generated character frames when detailed acting matters. Keep the same material treatment on subject and environment.

## Palette

A small contemporary editorial palette with clear value separation; use the client brand when relevant.

## Motion

Choose an actual performance such as a jump, reach or surprised recoil. Use anticipation, contact, compression and delayed secondary follow-through. Anchor squash and stretch at the feet or other support point.

## Transition

A concentric iris or bold shape reveal echoes the composition.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Do not stretch around the center and make feet slide through the ground. Large poses may need their own silhouettes rather than forcing a single rig. Stipple is surface material, not a reason to regenerate noise each frame.
