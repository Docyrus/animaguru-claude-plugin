# Decorative gold and geometric ornament

Gold leaf, spirals, patterned robes and selective naturalistic features.

## Material and construction

Combine polished gold texture with spiral and geometric pattern fields. Keep faces and hands more legible than the ornament. Draw patterns into an offscreen layer, clip once to the costume silhouette, then composite it back. Repeated source-in operations on individual motifs can erase earlier work.

## Palette

Gold, warm cream, black and restrained jewel accents; vary metallic value rather than scattering unrelated hues.

## Motion

Ornamental eyes blink, small leaves drift, a gold sweep crosses the robe and spiral ornament unfolds. Clothing follows the body anchors, with highlights clipped to the gold material.

## Transition

A spiral expands or gold ornament grows into the next frame.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Pattern clipping and hand attachment were weak in the source study. Ornament should not obscure faces. Do not wash paper color over a subject with source-atop; mask the texture and use the intended multiply blend instead.
