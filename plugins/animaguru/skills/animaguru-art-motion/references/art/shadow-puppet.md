# Chinese shadow-puppet theatre

Translucent carved leather, riveted joints, visible rods and warm cloth backlight.

## Material and construction

Create a cached cloth screen and prop layer. For each leather piece: fill, carve with destination-out inside a clip, restore a protective leather rim, then outline in deep brown around 2.6 px. Stamp a soft offset shadow followed by the multiply leather layer. Add rivets and rods. Keep carving on isolated offscreens so it does not cut holes in the final frame.

## Palette

Red #C0352A, green #2F7D4C, amber #D99A2E, yellow #E2BD4A and blue #2C5A8A against warm backlight.

## Motion

Step the main action near 10 fps. Rotate the head around its neck rivet, let a loose wrist and cup swing together, and delay each segment of a multi-jointed tail. A rod-operated butterfly flips its wings; lamp variation moves the cloth light pool.

## Transition

A backlight shift or articulated leather screen sweep.

## Failure cases and adaptation

White fur can be cut out, retaining only the leather edge. Multiply overlaps legitimately darken, but keep near-black overlaps away from the focal face or costume. Rods should travel down/out rather than cross other characters. Many offscreen multiply passes were expensive in the source.
