# Chiaroscuro and impasto light

Figures emerge from warm darkness through selective window and candle illumination.

## Material and construction

Paint a fully readable lit base with broad background strokes, finer props and modeled subjects. Multiply it by a lighting map: low ambient light plus broad window, face, chest, table and candle pools. Add subtle separation light behind the dark side of the subject. Soften lost edges with a blur/clear pair, preserving facial features. Place bristle impasto highlights only in lit regions.

## Palette

Deep umber ambient light, warm golden highlights and restrained red/cream costume notes.

## Motion

Candle flame leans and changes height with seeded continuous noise; light pools shift slightly, clouds dim the window, and dust appears only within the shaft. Let the subject perform one small action in the pool.

## Transition

An iris of light or darkness reveals another composition.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Three neat highlight stripes resemble stickers; use many irregular fine bristles and broken ridges. Recopying a rectangular crop restores unwanted unpainted background: keep props on separate layers. Dense grain dirties faces. Check hand → cup → thumb occlusion order.
