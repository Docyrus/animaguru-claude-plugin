# Surrealist soft forms

Hard light, long shadows and soft objects that violate their expected rigidity.

## Material and construction

Cache the setting; layer an animated shadow, props, warped object and subject. Render a long shadow into one mask before applying alpha/blur, avoiding dark overlaps between separate pieces. Deform every part of a soft clock through the same mapping: rim, dial, hands and indices must agree.

## Palette

Sky #22407C / #5F88BD / #E9DCAE, sea #8AA6B8 / #4F6F86, ground #D2AD70 / #7A5129 and aged gold.

## Motion

Let a clock visibly sag, ants travel a coherent path or a stilt-legged animal step with phased legs. A source clock used top compression .28–.32 and a pronounced sag over about 1.2 s. Round the droplet profile outward rather than pulling sharp cones down.

## Transition

Rounded liquid drops bring in the new image.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Warping only the outline leaves the clock hands rigid and breaks the illusion. Maintain ground contact and a consistent light vector. Generic flat vector subjects can weaken the painted world; prepare them in the same medium.
