# Watercolor background with cel characters

Layered watercolor environments, warm linework and observed natural motion.

## Material and construction

Displace polygon edges in several seeded midpoint passes, reducing amplitude by about .6 each pass. Layer transparent washes with tide-line edges. Example walls use five washes near .4 alpha and broad soft blooms; wood uses fewer tighter washes. Paint white highlights source-over rather than multiply. Cel subjects get a simple base, crescent shadow and warm brown contour.

## Palette

Natural greens, warm earth, translucent pale washes and clean light accents; avoid a uniformly moldy sepia cast.

## Motion

A gust bends the lower curtain edge, grass receives a traveling phase, clouds drift, and dappled light, hair and leaves share the same breeze. Cache static watercolor work and keep its seeds fixed.

## Transition

A wet-paper bloom or foreground foliage reveals the next scene.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Hard rectangular shadow seams and flat vector faces break the painted setting. Soften only what needs it. Apply compatible edge/material treatment to characters. The upstream background was stronger than its figures; do not treat that demo as finished character-quality evidence.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
