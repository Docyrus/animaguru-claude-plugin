# Dunhuang-inspired mineral mural

Mineral pigment, iron-wire contour, flying ribbons and aged plaster.

## Material and construction

Lay mineral colors over plaster with thin warm-dark contours, around 2.6 px in the source scale. Age the background with broad peeling masks, protecting faces and garments. Use translucent halos and flowing overhead ribbon arcs. Inspect historical reference for costume and iconography rather than inventing factual details.

## Palette

Mineral red #A5452F / #7A3020, green #3F8F6E / #6AB08E, blue #2F5F8F / #5A86B0, white #ECDFC4 and gold #D8B05A.

## Motion

A long ribbon travels through the scene, flowers rotate and fall, lamps flicker and a few plaster flakes descend. Give ribbons enough travel to be visibly flowing; an 18 px sway was ineffective compared with a much broader arc in the source.

## Transition

A flying ribbon sweeps across and becomes the next composition.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Fine random speckles look like dirt rather than broad plaster loss. Ageing on a body reads as damaged clothing. An opaque ring looks like a life buoy rather than a halo. Character anatomy remained a weaker part of the upstream demo.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
