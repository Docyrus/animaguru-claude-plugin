# Cave painting

Pigment, charcoal and animal silhouettes on a relief rock surface. Useful for origins, memory and prehistoric worlds.

## Material and construction

Cache a relief-like stone base with broad irregular light and dark patches. Draw charcoal silhouettes and rough ochre deposits over it; let pigment break at the rock grain. Keep the wall texture substantially larger than dust. Use an irregular drawn edge rather than a smooth vector outline.

## Palette

Earth ochre, charcoal, bone and muted red; separate the subject from the rock through value before adding grain.

## Motion

Use a two-pose animal run, fire flicker and rising embers. Let the torch change local illumination without regenerating the wall. A repeated action can continue across style changes using global film time.

## Transition

Reveal pigment along a hand-painted route or let torchlight uncover the next composition.

## Failure cases and adaptation

A modern cartoon face against a convincing cave wall breaks the medium. Simplify human figures into the same pigment language. Fire-driven light variation should not become full-frame random flicker.
