# Gothic illuminated manuscript

Vellum, embossed gold leaf, stained glass, ornamental borders and restrained marginal humor.

## Material and construction

Cache vellum, architecture, gold, borders and text columns. Make gold with low-frequency variation plus strongly horizontal polishing grain. A 46 px diamond grid with dark lines and highlights offset about 1.2 px suggests embossing. Build a gold-only mask from the painted regions or a material ID; sweep a diagonal light through that mask. Keep colored parchment out of the mask.

## Palette

Gold #9C6A1A / #D9A63A / #F8DE86, warm vellum, manuscript red and blue.

## Motion

Use a steady gold sweep, glints when it crosses raised decoration, swaying vines, stained-glass sparkle and a small marginal creature. A constant-speed sweep can place the glint more reliably than an easing curve that races past the subject.

## Transition

Turn a manuscript page or sweep gilding into the next scene.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Measure the actual text width; blackletter easily overflows narrow columns. Leaves need enough scale and density to read at delivery size. Keep gold sparkle localized, and measure local motion after any camera entrance settles.
