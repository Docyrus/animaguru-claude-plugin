# Vaporwave and VHS neon

Deliberate retro neon, perspective grids, banded sunset and analog-video distortion.

## Material and construction

Compose the scene offscreen, then add channel separation and tracking as a post layer. Neon tubes combine a broad blurred glow with a thin near-white core. Cut widening horizontal gaps from a gradient sunset. Use a projected grid with a stable vanishing point and distance-dependent line spacing; animate phase modulo the true repeat period.

## Palette

Magenta #FF4FD8, hot pink #FF2F8F, cyan #3FF6FF, deep purple #12052A / #2A0B4A, yellow #FFD84A and orange #FF8A3A. This palette is intentional for this treatment.

## Motion

Scroll the grid, drift sunset bands, flicker selected sign tubes and pass a bounded VHS tracking band. RGB offsets can start near 3 px with occasional larger glitches. Keep exact UI labels composed above destructive post effects when needed.

## Transition

A VHS tracking tear or signal interruption changes the shot.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Check glyph coverage for arrows and non-Latin neon signs. A garment too close to skin color can become unreadable. Outlining every component produces internal neon seams; derive an outer silhouette when that is the intended effect. Full-frame noise is not evidence of meaningful animation.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
