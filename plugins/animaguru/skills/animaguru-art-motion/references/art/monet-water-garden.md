# Water-garden impressionism

Water reflections, lilies, willow shadows and color-built light.

## Material and construction

Paint water in blue/lavender bands, then horizontal short strokes without outlines. Source water marks used cell 9, length 34 and width 6.5 px. Treat bridge, window and subject in separate finer passes so structure survives the foliage. A dark green backing behind the bridge and lighter green structural lines keep it visible. Use warm light and violet shade on characters.

## Palette

Water #5B6FB5 / #7D8FD0 / #9DB5E0 / #6AA0A8 / #B7A6D8, green leaf groups and pale pink/white flowers.

## Motion

Short horizontal light marks shimmer, lilies drift slowly, willow reflections wave and elliptical ripples expand. Wisteria should hang as clustered dots rather than smooth capsules. Keep floor and architecture static while water responds.

## Transition

A water ripple or reflection dissolve connects scenes.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Whole-screen 6 fps boiling became noisy in the source. Localize movement to water and foliage. Mixing a pond mural and a separate room can confuse space; choose a coherent composition for the new subject. Give text its own quiet backing.
