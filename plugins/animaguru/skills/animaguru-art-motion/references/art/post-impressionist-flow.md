# Post-impressionist flow strokes

Directional impasto and expressive color fields, including a Van Gogh-inspired treatment.

## Material and construction

Use a flat composition as a color source, then repaint in several passes with region-specific vector fields: vertical wall marks, perspective floor marks and star vortices. Give each region its own multi-color swatch. Dark stroke rims and small bright ridges create paint body. Process props and characters separately with finer marks, then restore visible features.

## Palette

Contrasting cobalt/ultramarine, warm yellow and ochre, with distinct regional color groups; the palette follows the chosen artwork or subject.

## Motion

Stars rotate through vortices, a lamp halo expands, vegetation sways and the main subject acts. Keep stroke locations stable while the flow field changes continuously. Cache static work and profile the full multi-pass renderer.

## Transition

A directional swirl carries paint into the next image.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Single-color jitter produces colored noise rather than expressive painting. Large brushes erase small props. Full-frame seed changes flash. The source implementation was among the expensive styles at roughly 110 ms/frame; that is historical context, not a current AnimaGuru benchmark.
