# Egyptian wall painting

Registers, profile silhouettes, mineral pigment and deliberate symbolic movement.

## Material and construction

Build a warm plaster ground, then horizontal registers, architectural borders and flat figures. Combine a profile head with a frontal eye and shoulders deliberately. Use fine consistent contours and repeating glyph bands; draw or source accurate symbols when their meaning matters.

## Palette

Sand and cream ground with mineral red, blue, green, black and restrained gold.

## Motion

A scarab pushes a sun disc; rays open like hands; a glyph band rewrites or scrolls. Articulated arms should turn at clear joints. Quantize decorative actions if a ceremonial stepped rhythm helps.

## Transition

A painted fabric band or vertical register wipes across the scene.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Do not treat decorative glyphs as a translated quotation. Stiff elbows can be improved with measured shoulder, elbow and wrist anchors. Keep the intentional frontal/profile convention consistent instead of drifting between views.
