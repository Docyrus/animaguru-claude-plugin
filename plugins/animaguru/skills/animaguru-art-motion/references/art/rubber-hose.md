# Rubber-hose cartoon

Pie-cut eyes, noodle limbs, white gloves, contact squash and old-film cadence.

## Material and construction

Cache a simple vintage setting. Use round faces, constant-width curved limbs and simplified gloves. Render the complete scene, then desaturate and apply a warm faded-paper tint consistently. Quantize character time to 12 fps while film wear can update at 24 fps.

## Palette

A controlled monochrome range with warm paper #F2E8D2; keep white gloves and props in the same treatment.

## Motion

Bounce around each object’s contact point on the chosen beat. Source squash used about 9% compression with inverse horizontal expansion and slight airborne stretch. Offset phases for secondary objects. Animate an actual pose, dancing flower or smoke ring before adding gate weave, scratches and dust.

## Transition

A rounded film-gate iris or a cartoon smoke transition.

## Failure cases and adaptation

A high motion percentage can come almost entirely from film noise; inspect clean character movement separately. Smooth angular body outlines where the cartoon needs curves. Apply monochrome treatment after all subjects, or individual props retain inconsistent color.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
