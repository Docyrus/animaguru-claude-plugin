# Expressionist waves

Long translucent currents, pressure in the sky and controlled whole-scene distortion.

## Material and construction

Integrate long flow-field strokes of roughly 200–300 px rather than short outlined impasto. Source examples use 26–42 steps, 6–8 px per step, 9–13 px brush width and alpha .5–.78. Keep the flat color below translucent strokes. Apply continuous row and column warps with overscan at least as large as the maximum displacement.

## Palette

Burning sky oranges/reds such as #E2541C, #F08A24, #C8301E and #B82A1E against cool water and dark structural masses.

## Motion

Let the sky undulate and the environment bend around the focal action. One source warp combines 9 sin(.012y+4.2t) and 4 sin(.031y−6t), with a smaller column warp. Scale all amplitudes to the output and desired emotional intensity.

## Transition

A downward pressure of color or a wave carries the cut.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Short dark-rimmed strokes make this look like the post-impressionist card. Thin translucent currents are the distinction. Overscan prevents exposed blank strips. Choose type that supports the mood rather than a comic handwritten default.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
