# Impressionist dabs

Broken light and short varied marks, distinct from uniform pointillist dots.

## Material and construction

Start from a readable color/value composition. Repaint regions with short ellipses and dabs sampled from local warm/cool palettes. Keep stroke positions seeded; vary their light or orientation smoothly. Use a finer separate pass for window frames, hands and facial landmarks. Avoid thick outlined strokes.

## Palette

Pastel regional palettes with warm sunlight and cool shadows; sample several colors per region rather than adding RGB noise to one base color.

## Motion

Dappled light crosses a surface, poppies or grass sway, clouds drift and a ribbon catches the breeze. Select a main action and supporting loops that fit the scene.

## Transition

Build the next image from dabs or let a field of marks carry the cut.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Uniform pure-color equal-size dots belong to pointillism. Whole-frame reseeding reads as flashing. Faces and hands were weak in the source demo: protect their contours and inspect a full-size still before adding more texture.
