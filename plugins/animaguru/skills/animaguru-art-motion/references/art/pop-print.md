# Pop-art print

Ben-Day dots, heavy ink contours, flat color and bold comic emphasis.

## Material and construction

Build repeatable dot textures as small pattern tiles. A 45-degree grid can use corner and center dots; example pitches are 24 px for a wall and 13 px for skin at 1080p. Cache the background. Draw flat pieces with roughly 5 px dark contours, white highlight slashes and clipped diagonal hatching. Treat ink and color registration deliberately.

## Palette

Ink #141414, paper #F3EEDB, blue #2F5CC8, yellow #F7D52C, red #D8232A; optional quadrant colors for a screen-print sequence.

## Motion

Cycle colors in a four-panel print, stagger thought-bubble dots, quantize an onomatopoeia burst and add one clear character anticipation. Keep the burst away from the face and essential evidence.

## Transition

A lightning-shaped halftone reveal or comic-panel cut.

## Failure cases and adaptation

A large yellow hair mass reads as cloth without distinct curls and internal C-shaped ink arcs. When a body crouches, move its head anchor with it. Screen-space and object-space pattern alignment are choices: avoid unintended texture swimming.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
