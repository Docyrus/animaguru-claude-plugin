# Polka dots and infinity space

Hand-irregular dot fields, repeating depth and ribbed organic forms.

## Material and construction

Use seeded irregular hexagonal grids with deliberate large and small dots. Squash floor spacing in perspective; build infinity lights as a coherent multi-depth grid rather than random bokeh. For a pumpkin-like form, align dot size and spacing to the ribs, largest near the central visible face.

## Palette

High-contrast dot/ground pairs, plus a restrained multi-color light grid when the scene calls for infinity-space depth.

## Motion

A radial wave can scale dots by .82+.28 sin(.012 distance−6t). Let a ribbed form breathe about its base and send a traveling wave through the dot field. Use the true two-row hex-grid period for scrolling.

## Transition

Dots grow to reveal the next image through a clipped mask.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Limit a transition dot radius relative to its spacing; around .47 of the gap avoids an uncontrolled whiteout in the source setup. Preserve subject/background contrast when both carry dots. Random bokeh alone loses the organized spatial idea.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
