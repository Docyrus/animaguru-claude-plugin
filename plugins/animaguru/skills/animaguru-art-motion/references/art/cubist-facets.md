# Cubist facets

Multiple viewpoints, angular silhouettes and overlapping planes.

## Material and construction

Start with structural facets large enough to explain the form. Layer translucent gradient planes; let selected facets extend past the original contour to change the silhouette. Use a coarse free-edge mesh rather than clipping a fine triangular overlay to an otherwise realistic object. A face can combine a profile and frontal plane intentionally.

## Palette

Muted ochre, brown, blue-gray and cream; keep a deliberate focal value structure.

## Motion

Shuffle letter fragments, slide window planes and let a paper facet tremble at a pinned edge. Animate a few structural planes, retaining recognizability through the change.

## Transition

Break or reassemble the picture into angular shards.

## Failure cases and adaptation

A low-poly filter alone does not express multiple viewpoints. Too many bright brown masses flatten the subject, an issue in the upstream demo. Keep facial landmarks and large planes readable, with no continuously random triangle reshuffle.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
