# Bauhaus geometry

Primary-color geometry, balanced spatial systems and articulated mechanical movement.

## Material and construction

Compose circles, rectangles, bars and grid modules with clear proportions and pivots. A Schlemmer-inspired geometric figure can share the same primitive vocabulary as the room. Cache the static geometry and retain stable joint locations. Let structure, balance and negative space carry the style.

## Palette

Cream/white, near-black, primary red, blue and yellow.

## Motion

A lever turns, a grid changes colors, a ball contacts a surface and articulated discs rotate around their joints. Use contact timing and mechanical cause-and-effect instead of unrelated floating shapes.

## Transition

A primary-colored geometric iris or moving bar reconfigures the composition.

## Failure cases and adaptation

A generic collection of circles is weak without a strong layout. A head whose pivot drifts disconnects from the body; inspect the neck through all poses. Do not add painterly distress or soft gradients by default.
