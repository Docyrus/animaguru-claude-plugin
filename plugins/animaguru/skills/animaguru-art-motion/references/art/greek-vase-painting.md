# Greek black-figure pottery

Terracotta, black silhouettes and incised internal detail, suited to friezes and mythic sequences.

## Material and construction

Lay a terracotta field over subtle fired-clay variation. Use black-figure silhouettes, pale incised lines and restrained maroon/white accents. Assemble meanders as repeatable border tiles. Internal incisions must survive the final viewing size, especially where dark forms overlap.

## Palette

Terracotta orange, near-black, cream and muted wine red.

## Motion

Counter-scroll the upper and lower meanders, let a ship travel across the frieze, and use a dolphin leap or lyre pluck as the main motif. Keep figures on a shared register rather than adding modern perspective.

## Transition

Roll the pottery surface or carry the next scene in a moving frieze.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Overlapping black bodies merge unless incised separation lines remain visible. Match the repeat period to the actual meander tile. A smooth shaded body looks like a contemporary illustration rather than ceramic decoration.
