# Blue-period painting

A restrained blue monochrome, elongated figures, broad dry strokes and slow emotional pacing.

## Material and construction

Paint a blue-only base. Use broad low-contrast strokes; source wall values were cell 34, length 110 and width 34 px, followed by a soft pass. Isolate small props instead of excluding rectangular patches from the stroke renderer. Elongate subjects around their feet and use exactly the same matrix for fills, texture and outlines.

## Palette

#132240, #1D3866, #2D5A94, #6B8FB6, #B6C8D8 and teal-gray #3B6878; contours near Prussian blue #0C1830.

## Motion

Use slow breathing, a deliberate hand action, half-speed steam, distant sea bands and moonlight fragments. Source breathing used sin(1.8t) with small amplitude; contours stay steady, without line boiling.

## Transition

Strip away chroma into blue or let a broad blue stroke reveal the scene.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Separate hair, skin and fur through value rather than leftover ochre. Short dense vertical marks look like rain or a blanket; keep strokes broad and quiet. Closely packed round caps can look like rows of heads. Mood and silhouette matter more than a blue color filter.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
