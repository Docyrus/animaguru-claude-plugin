# Cinematic anime light

Detailed environments, luminous summer skies, hard cel shadows and selective optical light.

## Material and construction

Cache architecture, then layer clouds, exterior details, glass, curtains, light patches, subjects and post effects. For a cel shadow, subtract a translated part silhouette from itself; reverse the shift for a narrow rim light. Cloud groups need a blue-gray common silhouette plus offset lit lobes, then soft/clear passes, rather than outlining every sphere.

## Palette

Warm skin #FFE8DC / #F2B6A8, cool white #FBFBFE / #B9C3DC, navy #2C3A66, saturated sky and warm sunlight.

## Motion

Clouds slowly develop, a contrail and birds cross, a glass droplet slides with a trace, and a curtain’s shadow follows its fabric. Let hair and leaves share the breeze. Cache blurred light wedges; use sparse dust and restrained quarter-resolution bloom.

## Transition

A diagonal light wipe or match cut along the bright sky.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Keep the sun inside the glass opening so flare does not appear to originate on the wall. Oversized horizontal flares resemble stray lines. Draw a droplet with transparency, a lower arc and a glint rather than an opaque bubble. The source had strong lighting but limited scene/character detail.
