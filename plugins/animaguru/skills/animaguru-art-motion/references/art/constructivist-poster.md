# Constructivist poster collage

Cream paper, red wedges, black geometry, photomontage and forceful type.

## Material and construction

Convert photos to gray halftone on a 45-degree grid. Radius can follow cell × gain × sqrt(1−luminance), batching dots into one path. Source cell/gain values were 4.6/.74 for faces and 7/.8 for background. Give cutouts a clear paper edge, then compose diagonals, circles and modular lettering.

## Palette

Cream #E9DFC4, red #C8231C / #8F1711, black #151311 and light paper #F4ECD6.

## Motion

Stamp words in with a short stagger, animate a red wedge as a speech or broadcast cone, and let slogans wave after landing. Keep a radio arc or one moving geometric element subordinate to the message.

## Transition

A diagonal red wedge, typographic bar or printed stamp changes the scene.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Oppositely wound letter components may cancel holes under nonzero fill; fill components separately where needed. Accents must fit the line box. Use glyphs with actual font coverage. Photo halftone is evidence texture only when the underlying material is authentic.

Parameters above are upstream study values at approximately 1920×1080 unless stated otherwise. Scale them to the delivery format and check the actual result. These are English production adaptations, not bundled renderers or claims of validated AnimaGuru implementations.
