# Art Nouveau

Organic whiplash curves, ornamental halos and integrated botanical decoration.

## Material and construction

Construct variable-width ribbons by offsetting both sides of a sampled centerline. Keep the exterior contour heavier than interior detail. Integrate flowers, hair, steam and framing into a continuous curve language. Use a darker offset silhouette for an outer contour rather than applying equal-width strokes everywhere.

## Palette

Muted botanical greens, cream, warm gold and a restrained rose or rust accent.

## Motion

An ornamental sunrise halo opens, flowers turn, long hair follows a gentle delayed arc and steam traces an S-curve. Attach stems and ribbons to real anchors so they seem to grow from the composition.

## Transition

Grow a botanical gold line into a frame or let an organic ribbon sweep across.

## Failure cases and adaptation

Uniform-width geometry loses the style. Do not cover the explanation with ornament. The source animal silhouette was a weak point: simplify and test it at thumbnail size before adding internal decoration.
