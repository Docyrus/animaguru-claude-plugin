# Concept morph and mathematical explanation

Choose this when a concrete object can become another object: a grid becomes a vector, weights become a sum, or a curve changes under a transformation. Write “A becomes B” for each passage. Use another grammar for a quotation, anecdote or launch with no meaningful transform.

## Visual structure

Put the object on screen first, then the question or formula. A dark, often black, ground and thin saturated lines support clear semantic color. Example colors are blue #58C4DD, red #FC6255, yellow #FFFF00 and teal #5CD0B3. Keep a concept’s color consistent in the diagram, legend and equation. Computer Modern-like mathematical typography can sit alongside a compatible reading face. Glow is optional emphasis, not the defining feature.

## Motion and timing

Use the quintic smoothstep 6p^5−15p^4+10p^3 for transforms. A 1–3 second change followed by roughly one second of reading is a useful starting point. Match point count, winding and starting landmark when interpolating paths. Stagger related objects slightly, preserving the relation between them. Write/reveal strokes by measured length; use a passing signal only along a real connection. During a reading hold, objects and camera can genuinely stop.

## Camera and transitions

Prefer object continuity: the end state is the next explanation’s starting state. A camera move targets the next meaningful object, usually over about a second. Before a scale traversal, identify the target, then interpolate zoom logarithmically and redraw vectors at that scale. The portal must cover the screen before the composition switches. Match formula symbols by identity rather than crossfading two whole equations.

## AnimaGuru adaptation and review

Use the existing kit’s analytic easing, line reveals and scene containers; implement missing path resampling locally. Upstream DG/CAM/MO names are not installed APIs. Check mathematical meaning as well as geometry: an output neuron connects to the preceding hidden layer, not directly to image pixels unless the actual architecture says so. Maintain the same value through a diagram, number label and conclusion. Give the final result time to be read. Do not turn successive bullet reveals into a black-background slide deck.
