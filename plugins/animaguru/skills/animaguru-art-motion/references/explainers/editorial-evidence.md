# Editorial evidence collage

Use when the viewer should inspect actual documents, photos, maps, dates or quotations. Place the argument in a coherent desk/wall world and guide attention through the evidence. For AnimaGuru’s established tactile brand treatment, also read the sibling animaguru-cutcraft skill when present; this card adds evidence-specific choreography.

## Visual structure

Let the material occupy most of the frame while it is being read; an expansive desk of tiny framed images weakens the evidence. Use static paper fibers, restrained shadows, original cutouts and a small annotation palette. Source study colors include paper #E4DCCF, ink #171716, red #B65255 / #93403C and muted highlighter yellow #DACF08. Use the client’s own identity. Exact quotations remain faithful to the source and are composed as real text or shown in an authentic capture.

## Motion and timing

Separate smooth camera time from stepped element time. Quantizing drawn lines, labels or cutouts to about 12 fps can suggest editorial handwork while the camera remains smooth. Photos may already be present on the cut; avoid making every item bounce or slide in. Source examples use red lines over .5–1 s, a hand-drawn open circle near 1 s and a highlighter following a spoken phrase over 1.5–3 s. Schedule by actual words and readability, not a fixed typing rate borrowed from Chinese text.

## Camera and transitions

Fit the camera to the exact evidence rectangle plus reading margin. Pan along a connecting line, push into the relevant sentence, pull out to reveal the full argument, or hard cut on a narrative boundary. One continuous world should keep one camera through connected shots. Tears are a possible paper effect but were not a defining transition in the source’s sampled Vox pieces. Keep paper still while it is being read.

## AnimaGuru adaptation and review

Store authentic evidence with URLs and dates; label generated illustrations as conceptual in production records. Use AnimaGuru for original art, not fabricated archives. Render important reading text above clips, pins and neighboring cards. Re-read all simultaneously visible text to catch accidental combined meanings. Perform an event once; pushing closer must not replay a completed acquisition or transaction. In portrait, crop to the relevant half-sentence and follow its word cue before revealing the next region. Check source fidelity, highlighter bounds, top-layer text and full-speed camera variety.
