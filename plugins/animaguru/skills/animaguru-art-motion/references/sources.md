# Sources, translation and scope

This skill is an English AnimaGuru adaptation of [Huashu Art Motion](https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion), by alchaincyf (Huashu), discovered through [the Voxyz_ai post](https://x.com/Voxyz_ai/status/2109690569563746755).

- Pinned source revision: `2571ee0a9a0467c4d99e935ea9e0fadda19210b0`.
- Reviewed on October 15, 2026.
- Upstream code and documentation: MIT, with the original copyright and license retained in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).
- Coverage: all 36 art recipe cards and all nine explainer grammar cards, mapped individually in [coverage.json](coverage.json).

The post described 35 art styles. At the pinned revision the repository has 36: its latest standalone SVG cartoon card has source ID `37_rick_morty`, while ID 07 has no card. The older 35-scene gallery and the SVG example use different render paths. Nine grammars are documented; eight have parameterized upstream examples, while the presenter card has a reference film. This skill bundles English reference guidance, not those engines, examples, characters or media.

## What was translated and adapted

The [art index](art-styles.md) links English cards for every source recipe. Each retains material construction, useful palette/parameter examples, motif movement, transition ideas and known limitations. The [grammar index](explainer-grammars.md) covers every source grammar's purpose, composition, choreography, camera, timing and common failures. Individual cards link back to the pinned upstream document.

Shared methods from the source's analysis, mechanism, still-first routes, code-rendering guide, rhythm/audio, lessons, author contract, grammar integration, character, long-scroll, narration and camera documents are consolidated in [production](production.md) and [rendering](rendering.md). This is a production translation and adaptation, not a line-for-line archive of every demo log or private review conversation.

English copy is fitted by real width, voice and reading time. Chinese glyph-count and typing-speed examples do not become English limits. Names, source IDs and original copyright attribution are preserved where they identify provenance. Source numerical values are marked as starting points from upstream studies; historical quality ratings and QA tables are not presented as current AnimaGuru test results.

## Deliberate AnimaGuru changes

Use the installed AnimaGuru generation tools, project assets, enabled models, cue sheet, PixiJS kit, player, audio tools and exporter. The upstream Canvas global APIs and author-specific speech endpoints are not installed or assumed. Generated layers, procedural art and hybrid production are all available decisions; advanced character quality should be judged from the actual result.

The reusable guidance does not import upstream promotional instructions, update checks, model-brand rankings, fixed demo coordinates, compulsory three-direction reviews, blanket character bans, hue bans, fixed motion percentages or automatic delegation. Those reflected the original author's environment or preferences. It preserves the evidence behind useful lessons: material-specific motion, deliberate cameras, stable random variation, correct sources and numbers, visible contact and honest review.

## Asset exceptions

The upstream README separates some assets from MIT code/documentation:

- Arphic-licensed stroke median data has its own terms.
- Bundled fonts have their own licenses, including OFL 1.1.
- The author's likeness, character sheets and footage containing that likeness are demonstration material.
- The Clawd mascot belongs to Anthropic; fan-demo footage is not general MIT media.
- Some museum artwork is separately designated CC0.

No such assets, voices, fonts, stroke data, footage or mascots are redistributed with this skill. The MIT notice does not authorize their reuse. Source styles referencing artists, studios or programs identify influences; use original or authorized subjects. When adding assets later, check their individual terms and preserve their notices.

## Extending the references
