# Constructivist Montage

Real archive footage, photographs and documents, printed as one ink-and-paper poster with a single red, cut on a machine beat: diagonal bands carry the type, windows hold the film, people are scissored out of photographs and stacked into structures. It is a documentary & broadcast style: real footage, photographs and documents carry the film. Subject, audience, emotional tone and story come from the user's brief. This style supports Explainer, Advertisement and General Video.

## Establish direction

Follow sibling `animaguru-video` for purpose → style → compact interview before new production. Reuse explicit choices and completed briefs; honor a requested interview skip. Share [the style gallery](https://animaguru.com/animation-styles#kino-montage). Fold missing visual questions into the shared interview:

- Which archive footage, photographs and documents (with rights) tell this story, and what is the signature build (a tower, a bridge span, a crowd forming a word)?
- What is the axis angle and tempo for the diagonal bands and the beat grid?

## Use real evidence

Ask the user for their own film, video, photographs, scans or data first; otherwise find licence-clean sources (see the [production recipe](references/recipe.md)). Never stand a generated image in for archive material, and never invent a number. Keep `production/FACTS.md` (every number, date and claim, with its source and quoted wording) and `production/CREDITS.md` (every asset's source, author and licence), delivered with the film. CC BY-SA or "no known restrictions" are not usable without the user's explicit agreement.

## Build the look and movement

Read `pixijs` and the relevant Filters, Graphics, Text or Container skills. Read `animaguru` before generating assets: generation can supply music or narration, never the archive imagery itself. Discover current models and estimates, respect the user's budget, and reuse completed media. Essential counters, headings and marked facts belong in authored canvas content.

- Four elements only — bands, windows, cut-outs, type blocks — share one ink-to-paper print grade; red is the only colour, laid on top, and never sits on red.
- Cuts conflict in direction, value or geometry on a beat grid that can halve per section; nothing holds longer than a second without something moving.
- The signature photomontage tower (or equivalent build) climbs one unit per beat with the count; a document's own drawings can be "lifted off the page" and restood to scale as a chart.

Use the [production recipe](references/recipe.md) for sources, the bundled original study and port-specific decisions. It is a technique example, not a prescribed film. Keep motions a function of absolute seconds; separate drawing cadence from camera/light cadence when the material calls for it. Prepare assets locally, preserve async seeking and register actual resource/cue times. Keep essential type editable and localized.

## Review and delivery

Inspect the print grade's consistency per clip, red never sitting on red, counter-to-caption agreement, type clear of window edges, and every number against `FACTS.md`. Check representative full-resolution frames, moving playback, desktop/mobile fit and cold seeks. Follow `animaguru-video` for production records, audio mixing and MP4 delivery. Describe the actual 2D/2.5D rendering method accurately. Physically rendered 3D remains deferred.
